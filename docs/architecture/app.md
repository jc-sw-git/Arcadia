# Architecture: `src/Arcadia.App`

The App's classes; the other projects are in [README.md](README.md).

Avalonia 12 UI (MVVM, CommunityToolkit.Mvvm 8.4 partial properties), `Theme/Theme.axaml`, `Views/MainWindow.axaml`, `ArcadiaUpdateService` (Velopack), `Services/LauncherLogos` (the user's logos copied as PNG into `%APPDATA%\Arcadia\logos`, the launchers' own as shapes) with `LauncherBadge` (logo, picture or letter tile) drawn by `Controls/LauncherLogo` wherever a launcher is named, `Services/SystemDialogs` (Windows' file and folder dialogs; opened from the controller they switch the mouse mode on, #138). `MainViewModel` routes each press to the topmost layer (first start or settings, else a dialog, else the page on top; what a page leaves, ≡ and X, opens the settings or the search), keeps the rules between pages, holds the dialogs and wires the parts (#155, #210). Pages are `IPage`s and ask Main through `IPageHost` to close, show a game's menu or the shared filter dialog: `LibraryViewModel` (the cover grid of the selected tab, its pages, focus, view and sort, and `EmptyTabViewModel` for a tab without games or whose launcher is gone, or no launcher active, #136; it lies under every page and asks more through `ILibraryHost`), `GameDetailViewModel` (detail carousel, `Views/GameDetailView.axaml`), the search, the settings and the first start. `LibrarySession` loads and rescans the launchers and follows installs and running games, handing what it read to Main (`ILibrarySessionOwner`) and the states to the covers; `GameActions` is what a game does (start, install, back to it, held A), one per window; `LauncherTabsViewModel` the tabs; `StatusBarViewModel` (header), `ToastViewModel`, `GameMenuViewModel` (context menu); `Setup/` holds the first start's page (#139): `FirstRunViewModel` with `FirstRunLauncherViewModel` rows, drawn by `Views/FirstRunView`; `Search/` holds `SearchViewModel` (search page, which takes all its input, #209, and its keyboard's `IKeyboardTarget`; hits shown in `HitSlotViewModel` places that keep their controls when the hit changes); `Keyboard/` holds the one on-screen keyboard (#110): `OnScreenKeyboardViewModel` (drawn by `Views/OnScreenKeyboardView`) with `BoardKeyViewModel`, `KeyboardLevels` (shift/AltGr), `LayoutMenuViewModel`, `IKeyboardTarget` (what it types into; its user greys out or leaves out keys, `BoardKeyState`) and `WindowInFrontTarget` (the mouse mode's); `Settings/` holds the settings page (#15): `SettingsPageViewModel` (categories, LB/RB, B), one `SettingsCategoryViewModel` per category (`LauncherSettingsViewModel` with `LauncherRowViewModel`s and its `ILauncherSettingsHost`, #136, and a page per launcher with `LauncherPageRows`, #137; `ControllerSettingsViewModel` with SISR's prompts as its `Dialog`, `AboutSettingsViewModel`; a new category is a subclass added in `MainViewModel.ShowSettings`), rows (`SettingsRowViewModel`: `ChoiceRowViewModel`, `SwitchRowViewModel`, `ActionRowViewModel`, `InfoRowViewModel`, `TextRowViewModel` with `TextEditorViewModel`, a text written with the on-screen keyboard and checked while typed; each with its `Info` as `InfoBlock`s for the info panel, optionally a `Preview` box above it and a `Lamp` beside the title), `SettingsChanges` (saves at once, reports a failed save); `Views/TabStrip` (launcher tabs and categories: centred, faded, wheel); `Controls/` holds `ButtonTextBlock` (buttons drawn framed inside a text), `KeyHint`/`KeyText` (a hint or label showing the controller's button or the keyboard's key, after `Input/InputHints`; `Input/GamepadRouting` takes the controller's buttons to `MainViewModel`, ⧉/≡ through `SoloButtons`), `HoldRing`, `RevealPanel` (the sliding keyboard), `SharedWidthRow` and `CollapsingStack` (the shrinking chips), `CentredDockPanel` (a key's name centred between icon and button); mouse mode: `Services/MouseModeHost` (wiring, overlay, automation), `Services/ForegroundGame` (also what is in front after a game ended, #94), `Services/GameWindow` ("Zurück ins Spiel"), `Services/ForegroundWindow` (its bounds and caret), `Views/MouseModeKeyboardWindow` with `Views/MouseModeKeyboardLegend` (the keyboard outside Arcadia, #110), `Views/MouseModeOverlay` (own non-activating window, clicks pass outside its region) with `Controls/ScreenEdgeFill`
